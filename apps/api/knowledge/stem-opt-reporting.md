# STEM OPT reporting requirements
Source: SEVP Study in the States guidance on STEM OPT reporting, as
published by university international offices. Confirm current
details on studyinthestates.dhs.gov and with your DSO.

While on the 24-month STEM OPT extension, a student has reporting
duties that do not apply during the first 12 months of OPT. Missing
them can put F-1 status at risk.

Validation reports every six months:
- A validation report confirms that the student's SEVIS record is
  correct. It covers legal name, residential or mailing address,
  employer name and address, and current employment status.
- Reports are due 6, 12, 18 and 24 months after the start date of the
  STEM OPT extension. Count from the start date on the STEM OPT EAD
  card, not the earlier OPT card.
- The report is required even when nothing has changed.
- Students generally submit it to their DSO within 10 days of the due
  date, using their school's form or portal. Schools handle the
  process differently, so follow your school's instructions.
- The SEVP Portal usually sends a reminder email 30 days before a
  report is due.

Self-evaluations on Form I-983:
- The initial self-evaluation is due at 12 months and the final one at
  24 months after the STEM OPT start date. A final evaluation is also
  due when employment ends before the 24 months.
- The student and the employer both sign it. The DSO must receive it
  no later than 10 days after the end of the training period.
- The 12-month and 24-month reports include both the validation report
  and the self-evaluation.

Changes to report within 10 days:
- A change of legal name, residential or mailing address, or employer
  name or address.
- Loss of employment.
- Material changes to the training plan, such as a change of the
  employer's EIN, a pay cut that is not tied to fewer hours, a
  significant drop in weekly hours, or changes to the training goals
  in the I-983. These require an updated Form I-983.

Unemployment: students on STEM OPT may not accrue more than 150 days
of unemployment in total across the OPT and STEM OPT periods.
