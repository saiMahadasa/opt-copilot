# Optional Practical Training (OPT)
Source: SEVP/ICE guidance and 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(ii)

Optional Practical Training (OPT) is one of the two types of temporary
employment available to students in F-1 status. It allows students to gain
practical experience in their field of study. The regulation defines it as
"temporary employment for practical training directly related to the
student's major area of study."

Eligibility:
- Available to students who have been in legal, full-time F-1 status for at
  least one academic year.
- Does not require a job offer to apply.
- A student may qualify for a new 12-month OPT period after completing a new
  degree at a higher educational level.
- Twelve months or more of full-time CPT eliminates eligibility for the
  one-year OPT period at that same educational level. Part-time CPT does not
  affect OPT eligibility.

Three types of OPT:
1. Pre-completion OPT, used before finishing a degree.
2. Post-completion OPT, used after finishing a degree, generally 12 months.
3. The 24-month STEM OPT extension, available only to students with a
   qualifying STEM degree working for an E-Verify employer (see stem-opt.md).

Filing window: students must submit Form I-765 for post-completion OPT no
earlier than 90 days before their program end date, and no later than 60 days
after the program end date.

The 90-day unemployment rule: during the initial 12-month OPT period, a
student is allowed a total of 90 days of unemployment. Exceeding 90 days of
unemployment is a violation of OPT status and can put F-1 status at risk.
Regulatory basis: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(ii)(E).

Travel: re-entering the US is possible before OPT is approved, but is not
recommended before receiving the EAD card and confirming employment. If
traveling with a pending application, students should carry the I-765 receipt
notice as proof of the pending application.
